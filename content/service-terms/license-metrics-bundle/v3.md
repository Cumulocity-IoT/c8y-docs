This agreement is made between {{< company-c8y >}} ("Provider") and the customer ("Customer") who utilizes the Provider's hosted software solutions and services. The following license metrics define the usage measurements and calculations that form the basis for billing and invoicing of {{< company-c8y >}} services.

These license metrics are valid for November 23rd, 2026 onwards.

### Differences from previous versions {#differences-v3}

Version 3 updates the [deployment](#deployment-v3) options. In all other respects, version 3 is identical to [version 2](#differences-v2).

### Deployment {#deployment-v3}

A unique instance of the hosted software ("Software") provided by the supplier ("Supplier") and made accessible to the Customer. Deployments can be "Standard Dedicated Environment", "Advanced Dedicated Environment" or "Public Shared Cloud" instances.
* **Standard Dedicated Environment:** An environment with dedicated resources hosted on a {{< company-c8y >}}-selected hyperscaler and region.
* **Advanced Dedicated Environment:** An environment with dedicated resources, with high availability through an active setup across three data centers; Customer-selected update schedule, hyperscaler, and geographic region.
* **Public Shared Cloud:** One or multiple tenants on a shared cloud environment, with configurable data retention.

{{< product-c8y-iot >}} Advanced Dedicated Cloud deployment environments can be used for development or testing ("Non-Production") purposes but are restricted in the scope of their usage. In this case, the Advanced Dedicated Cloud deployment environments may not be used for the processing of live production data or any purposes other than development or testing purposes only. A Non-Production Advanced Dedicated Cloud deployment environment may be subject to separate fees as written in the contract between {{< company-c8y >}} and the Customer. Note that {{< product-c8y-iot >}} Standard Dedicated and Shared Cloud deployments are always rated at the same rate regardless of instance type.
