The Connect Smartphone modal, which allows users to quickly connect their smartphones to Cumulocity IoT, was not opening when accessed from the Device Management Quick Links widget. This issue has been resolved, and users can now successfully open the Connect Smartphone modal by clicking on the corresponding link in the Quick Links widget. This fix ensures a smoother user experience when connecting smartphones to the platform, enabling users to efficiently manage their devices within the Cumulocity IoT application.
