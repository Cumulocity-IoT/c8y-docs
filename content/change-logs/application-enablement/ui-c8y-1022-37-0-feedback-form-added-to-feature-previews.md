To improve the quality and user experience of new features, a feedback form has been added to feature previews in Cumulocity IoT. When a user opens the feature preview drawer, they will now see a feedback form at the bottom of each feature that allows them to provide their thoughts, suggestions, and any issues encountered. This valuable feedback will help shape and refine the features before they are officially released, ensuring they meet the needs and expectations of our users. Submitting feedback is optional and does not impact the usage of the preview features.
