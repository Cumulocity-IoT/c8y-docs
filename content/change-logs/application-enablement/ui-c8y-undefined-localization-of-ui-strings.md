We are pleased to announce that {{< product-c8y-iot >}} has moved to AI-powered automation for translating our entire platform UI from English into all [supported languages](/get-familiar-with-the-ui/user-settings/#to-change-the-language-of-the-ui).

In addition, a regular review is carried out by professional translators for German, French, and Japanese.

**What this means for you**

- Faster availability of new features in your preferred language
- More consistent terminology across the platform

**Help us improve**

While AI technology continues to advance, we recognize the importance of human expertise in ensuring accurate and contextually appropriate translations. We value your insights and encourage you to share feedback and suggestions for improving translations through our [product support](/additional-resources/contacting-support/).

Your feedback will directly contribute to enhancing the translation quality of {{< product-c8y-iot >}} for all users worldwide.
