### Yearly release {#yearly-release}

Yearly releases are provided in a yearly cycle and named after the year, for example, {{< product-c8y-iot >}} 2025 release. The [General Availability (GA)](#ga) for a Yearly release is scheduled for the 31st of March of the respective year. See also, [Continous Deployment model](#continuous-deployment).
