The **Environments** page provides a high-level summary of each environment associated with your account.

<img src="/images/console/environments.png" alt="Environments">

In the above example, the customer has four environments under their account. Each environment can be viewed by clicking the respective tab at the top of the page, where a variety of details are shown, including the domain, tenant ID, and the provisioning date.

Under the **Subscribed add-ons** section, you can view which add-ons have been subscribed to that specific environment. For the preview, add-ons are subscribed manually by {{< company-c8y >}}. If you want to add a new add-on to an environment, reach out to your Customer Success Manager or Account Executive, or contact [product support](/additional-resources/contacting-support/). A short description of each add-on is available.

Each environment gets a randomly generated name upon onboarding or creation. You can provide a name and description for each environment by navigating to the respective tab at the top of the page.
