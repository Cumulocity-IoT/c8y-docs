The **Overview** page of the Console application serves as the homepage and contains a variety of information.

<img src="/images/console/overview-page.png" alt="Overview page">

This page contains the following sections:

* **Usage** - Statistics showing your consumption metrics including messages, devices, storage (GB), CPUs, and database usage, with trend indicators.
* **Commit to consume** - Details of the CTC contract, including the start and end dates and the total commitment value. The section displays a pie chart showing consumed versus available commitment.
* **Environments** - A list of all the environments that are associated with the account.
