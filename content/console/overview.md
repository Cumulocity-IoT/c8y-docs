The **Overview** page of the Console application serves as the homepage and contains a variety of information.

<img src="/images/console/overview.png" alt="Overview">

This page contains the following widgets:

* **Featured Add-on** - A widget showcasing one of the add-ons offered by {{< company-c8y >}} that are available for your environment.
* **Invoices** - A placeholder widget that will contain Consumption and Drawdown information in a future version of the Console application.
* **Commit to consume** - A widget providing the details of your CTC contract, specifically the start/end dates and the total commitment value. 
* **Environments** - A list of all of the environments that are associated with your account.
